Rent, sorted for everyone under one roof.

MyPGRent puts landlords and residents on the same ledger — invoices, UPI payment approvals, maintenance tickets, and building notices, tracked from one app instead of scattered across calls and chat threads.

Free to download for landlords and residents.

One app, two very different Sunday mornings.

A landlord checking on six properties and a resident waiting on a repair shouldn't need the same nine taps to get an answer. MyPGRent gives each side exactly the view they need.

For landlords

See your whole portfolio at a glance

Occupancy, total collected, and pending dues sit right on the home screen, alongside property, room, bed, and tenant counts pulled live from every building you manage.

  • Automated rent cycles, dues tracking, and collection rates
  • Expense logging across properties
  • Rent payment activity as it comes in
For residents

A verified pass for every resident

Room allocation, monthly rent, and emergency contact details live on one shareable digital pass — the thing a resident actually needs to hand over or pull up on request.

  • Share pass instantly when it's asked for
  • Emergency contacts kept one tap away
  • House rules and Wi-Fi details in the same place
For everyone

Rent payments that settle themselves

Residents see the full invoice breakdown and pay via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or bank transfer — then submit the UTR reference so the landlord can verify and approve it instantly.

  • Itemized dues: rent, electricity, water, maintenance
  • Landlord's UPI ID or QR code, set up once
  • A running receipt ledger for every payment
For landlords

Properties, floors, rooms, beds — organized

Built for shared PG and hostel accommodation as much as single-unit rentals: every floor, room, and bed carries its own occupancy status and allocation.

  • Multiple properties in one view, with revenue per building
  • Room-level occupied / available status
  • Bed-level allocations for shared rooms
For everyone

Maintenance tickets that don't go quiet

A resident logs a complaint with photos and a priority; the landlord moves it through a four-step tracker so both sides can see exactly where it stands.

  • Photo proof attached to every ticket
  • Waited, in progress, delivery, complete
  • Landlord response visible on the ticket itself

And everything else that keeps a building running

Notice board & templates

Broadcast building-wide notices using pre-built templates, and keep house rules current for every resident.

UPI & bank transfer setup

Upload a custom QR code, or configure an auto-extracted UPI ID and bank details once for the whole property.

Rules, documents & receipts

Residents can pull up landlord announcements, curfew timings, visitor guidelines, and past payment receipts anytime.

Built around a rent ledger, not a chat thread

Every invoice, payment reference, complaint, and notice lives against the property and resident it belongs to — so nothing depends on someone remembering to forward a screenshot.

No card or account numbers handled by the appPayments happen inside the resident's own UPI or banking app; MyPGRent only records the reference and status.
Data organized by propertyA resident only ever sees their own room, invoices, and notices — never another resident's.
